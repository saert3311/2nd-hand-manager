## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a boutique re-commerce visual identity bridging verified physical curation with agile digital discovery. Built for a modern omnichannel marketplace with roots in Alfafar (Valencia), the experience combines the velocity of contemporary circular marketplaces with the meticulous reassurance of certified technical inspection.

### Emotional Demeanor & Positioning
- **Trust & Assurance (Confianza Verificada):** Avoids flea-market visual clutter. Every piece feels audited, photographed with editorial precision, and cataloged with clear condition metrics.
- **Dynamic Opportunism (Oportunidad Inmediata):** Clear, legible urgency without aggressive flash-sale tropes. Badges communicate states like "Destacado", "Reservado", or "Vendido" with structural clarity.
- **Mediterranean Clarity:** Crisp light surfaces, deep teal anchor tones, warm amber accents, and clean borders evoke an uncluttered, modern Spanish retail studio.

### Design Movement: Modern Hybrid Functionalism
The system fuses the clean listing scannability of contemporary European circular platforms with high-legibility product audit cards. Layouts prioritize rapid price evaluation, verified item grading, instant direct-inquiry triggers (such as in-store pickup or direct messaging), and frictionless physical-to-digital inventory sync.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast readability against crisp, sunlit surfaces while establishing unmistakable semantic states across verified pre-owned lots.

### Color Palette Architecture
- **Primary Teal (`#0F766E` / Base `#0D9488`):** The hallmark of inspection, refurbishment integrity, and sustainable commerce. Applied to primary CTAs, active catalog navigation, certified check icons, and key interactive highlights. Soft teal tint (`#F0FDFA`) serves as the foundation for guaranteed verification containers.
- **Secondary Amber (`#D97706` / Tint `#FEF3C7`):** The opportunity signal. Reserved strictly for editorial flags, "Destacado" highlights, price drops, and "Reservado" transaction states.
- **Tertiary Channel Green (`#128C7E` / Accent `#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate omni-channel contact, WhatsApp reserve actions, and physical store inventory checks.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - **Surface Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` providing soft contrast against the pure `#FFFFFF` item card surfaces.
  - **Deep Slate Body (`#0F172A`):** Ensures maximum typographic crispness for technical specs and bold Euro prices.
  - **Muted Slate (`#64748B`):** Handles secondary specifications, condition caveats, and historical timestamps.
  - **Neutral Borders & Inactive States (`#E2E8F0` / `#475569`):** Subdued boundaries and archived/sold-out treatments.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes fast numerical evaluation and distinct hierarchy. `Outfit` provides confident, contemporary display forms with geometric balance, while `Hanken Grotesk` anchors high-density catalog descriptions, condition audits, and physical store logistics.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Euro Price Formatting:** Always render numerical values in `Outfit` with weight `700`, trailing the Euro sign (`149 €` or `1.250 €`), utilizing non-breaking spaces. When presenting original compared retail prices, apply strike-throughs using `body-sm` in `#64748B`.
- **Spanish Syntactical Calibration:** Labeling, filter tags, and physical store indicators remain in natural castellano (`Alfafar Store`, `Disponible en tienda`, `Garantía Verificada`, `Estado: Como nuevo`).
- **Product Title Scannability:** Product listing titles clamp to 2 lines max using `headline-sm`, ensuring identical baseline alignment across catalog grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an adaptive fluid system optimized for thumb-driven mobile scanning during commute hours and expansive multi-column desktop browsing.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Product lists adopt a compact 2-column card presentation or full-width detailed cards with persistent bottom sticky actions.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Allows split view between sticky map/store pick-up info and 2-to-3 column inventory grids.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max-width. Facilitates persistent left-hand facet filtration (3 columns) paired with a responsive 9-column item card gallery (3 cards per row).

### Spacing Discipline
All component internals operate on strict multiples of `4px` (`0.25rem`). Product badge insets, image gallery margins, and specification rows consistently use `space-sm` (`8px`) and `space-md` (`16px`) to preserve rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through low-contrast perimeter definition paired with tinted ambient under-glows, keeping the catalog tactile yet clean.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Canvas foundation (`#F8FAFC`). Flat filters and non-interactive item detail panels blend directly into this surface.
2. **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Background `#FFFFFF` bordered by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Features a delicate ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Card Lift):** Triggered on desktop cursor hover over active products: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. The subtle teal tint introduces branded elevation without visual noise.
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Trays & Modals):** Persistent WhatsApp inquiry bar, physical location drawer, and filter sheets: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed with top border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, precision-tailored corner geometry (`roundedness: 1`), conveying technical reliability, physical product craftsmanship, and architectural stability.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro-badges, technical spec chips, and internal image thumbnail corners.
- **Cards & Input Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Standard for catalog cards, search inputs, modal containers, and store pickup selectors.
- **Pill Geometry (Full Radius / `9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic category filters, item status labels ("Destacado", "Reservado", "Reacondicionado"), and primary WhatsApp floating action triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Compra / Reserva Online):** Deep Teal background (`#0F766E`), white text, bold `label-md`. Height `44px` on mobile, `40px` on desktop. Border radius `4px`. Hover state deepens to `#115E59`.
- **Channel Contact (WhatsApp Alfafar Direct):** WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`), white bold text, accompanied by the WhatsApp mark. Height `44px`. Focus states utilize a `#128C7E` outline.
- **Secondary / Secondary Ghost:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, slate text (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions to `#F8FAFC` and border `#CBD5E1`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Destacado / Oportunidad:** Amber tint (`#FEF3C7`), text `#D97706`, `1px solid #FDE68A`. Rounded pill shape. Font `label-sm`.
- **Verificado / Garantía:** Teal tint (`#F0FDFA`), text `#0F766E`, `1px solid #CCFBF1`. Accompanied by a verified check icon.
- **Reservado:** Slate tint (`#F1F5F9`), text `#475569`, `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Vendido (Archived):** Solid slate surface (`#E2E8F0`), text `#64748B`, grayscale treatment applied to associated product imagery.

### Product Catalog Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white card container with `0.5rem` outer boundary. Image aspect ratio 4:3 with light-neutral framing (`#F1F5F9`). Badges sit floating top-left with `8px` offset.
- **Information Sequence:**
  1. Micro-row: Category & Condition rating (e.g. "Vintage Audio • Estado 9/10").
  2. Title: 2 lines maximum, `headline-sm`, slate `#0F172A`.
  3. Price block: Current price in `price-display` Teal or Slate, crossed-out original benchmark.
  4. Availability footer: Visual dot indicating "En tienda Alfafar" or "Envío 24/48h".

### Form Controls & Filters
- **Interactive Search:** Soft off-white interior (`#F8FAFC`), transition to pure white on focus with `2px solid #0F766E`. Clear iconography representing search and location.
- **Pill Filters:** Horizontal scrolling container without visible scrollbars on touch devices. Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` with border `#E2E8F0`. Active state: `#0F766E` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Teal accent (`#0F766E`) checked filling with crisp white glyph. Outer border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.

### In-Store Physical Assurance Module
- Persistent location card communicating store presence in Alfafar (Valencia), operating hours, live reservation hold duration (e.g., "Reserva sin compromiso durante 24 horas y prueba en tienda"), and one-tap directions via native maps.
